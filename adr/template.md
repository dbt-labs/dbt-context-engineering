# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, the constraints, and what makes the decision non-obvious. State
facts, not the decision. A reader should feel the tension that the decision resolves.

## Decision

The decision, stated in active voice ("We will ..."). Be specific enough that someone can tell
whether the code conforms to it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Include the costs and the follow-on work,
not just the benefits. Link related ADRs with their number.
